# B听正式资产生成记录

工具：内置 imagegen。母图为已选 B 概念图。生成的 PNG 原样保留，平台资源只做尺寸适配和容器封装。

## 透明角色

```text
Use case: background-extraction / logo-brand production asset
Input image: the attached B听 B-companion concept board is the approved identity reference.
Create ONE production-ready isolated mascot, not a presentation board. Reproduce the approved friendly cream-colored uppercase B-shaped character with TWO closed smiling navy eyes, round coral-pink over-ear headphones arching over the head, and one small mint-cyan accent on the LEFT ear cup. Preserve the exact recognizable B-shaped silhouette and proportions of the large icon in the reference. Smooth clean flat graphic, vector-like edges, no 3D, no paper texture, no gradients, no shine, no cast shadow.
Output: square 1024x1024 transparent PNG with REAL alpha transparency everywhere outside the mascot and inside any cutout gaps; no background square, no frame, no mockup, no text, no watermark, no checkerboard painted into image. Center the complete mascot with the artwork occupying approximately 76% of canvas width and 76% height; all headphones and body fit with generous transparent margins. Navy outline #152342 sufficiently clean to read on cream and navy backgrounds, cream body #FFF4DE, coral headphones #F58F9C, small cyan detail #8BD8D8. Only the mascot, no separate icon sizes. It will be packaged into Android adaptive icons, Flutter splash and empty states; simple silhouette must remain legible at small sizes.
```

## 方形图标

```text
Use case: compositing / logo-brand
Use attached transparent production mascot as the exact character. Create ONE flat square Android app icon master, 1024x1024. Preserve the original mascot's exact B silhouette, two closed navy eyes, cream body, coral headphones, cyan accent; do not redesign, add limbs or add lettering. Place it centered on a completely opaque, flat uniform midnight navy #152342 background extending to all four edges. Mascot occupies about 72% of icon width and height, with spacious equal margins. Square canvas, NO rounded corner clipping (Android will apply its own mask), no device frame, no app name, no slogan, no watermark, no border, no shadows, no texture, no glow. No additional elements. This is a production icon, not a presentation.
```
